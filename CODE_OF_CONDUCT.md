Code of Conduct

Our Promise

We want AI Mock Interview to be a friendly, respectful, and welcoming community for everyone.

All contributors are expected to treat each other with respect.

Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and polite.
- Welcome beginners and experienced developers.
- Accept constructive feedback.
- Help others learn and improve.
- Focus on improving the project.
- Communicate professionally.

Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or hateful comments.
- Insulting or disrespecting contributors.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Spamming issues or pull requests.

Reporting Problems

If you experience or observe unacceptable behaviour, contact the project owner through GitHub.

Responsibility

The project owner may remove inappropriate comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary.

Our Goal

Let's build a supportive community and make interview preparation better for everyone.
