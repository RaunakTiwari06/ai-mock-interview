About

AI Mock Interview is a platform designed to help college students and freshers prepare for job interviews using artificial intelligence.

Our goal is to make interview preparation easier, smarter, and more accessible.

Features

- AI-powered mock interviews
- Personalised interview questions
- Voice-based interaction
- Interview performance feedback
- Communication improvement
- Posture and eye-contact analysis (planned)

Who Can Use It?

- College students
- Freshers
- Job seekers
- Internship applicants
